## About MediPraxis

**MediPraxis** is a digital practice management solution designed to streamline and simplify daily clinical workflows for independent healthcare practitioners and small clinics.

Healthcare professionals often struggle with:

- Fragmented tools
- Manual and time-consuming processes
- Poor data organization
- Security risks when sharing patient information

MediPraxis solves these problems by providing:

- A **single unified platform**
- **Secure patient data handling**
- **AI-assisted workflows**
- **Flexible and customizable tools**

The system is built with an **empathy-driven, practitioner-centered approach**, ensuring ease of use and adaptability to real-world healthcare environments.

## Core Features

---

### Appointment Scheduling

Efficiently manage appointments with:

- Appointment slot creation (recurring & ad-hoc)
- Calendar views (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Direct scheduling by practitioners
- Patient self-booking via web (no account required)
- Appointment reminders

**Benefits:**

- Reduces manual coordination (calls/messages)
- Improves scheduling efficiency
- Minimizes missed appointments

---

### Client / Patient Management

Centralized patient management system:

- Create and manage patient profiles
- Store contact details and demographics
- Quickly search and retrieve patient information
- Maintain structured patient data

**Benefits:**

- Eliminates scattered records
- Improves organization and accessibility

---

### Client / Patient Records & Reports

Secure medical record handling:

- Store prescriptions, diagnoses, and treatment notes
- Upload and manage patient reports
- Request reports from patients via secure links
- Maintain complete patient history
- Easy search and retrieval of past records

**Benefits:**

- Replaces insecure sharing methods (e.g., WhatsApp)
- Ensures continuity of care
- Keeps all records in one place

---

### Task & Workload Management

Manage daily clinical workload:

- Create, update, and delete tasks
- Link tasks to patients
- Track daily activities and reminders

**Benefits:**

- Reduces cognitive load
- Improves productivity
- Keeps practitioners organized

---

### Customizable Forms

Flexible form system tailored to different practices:

- Create custom forms based on practitioner needs
- Edit and reuse templates
- Three main form types:
  - Patient information forms
  - Medical record forms
  - Report request forms
- Prebuilt templates for common specialties

**Benefits:**

- Adapts to workflows instead of forcing change
- Saves time on repetitive documentation

---

### Communication & Report Sharing

Secure communication features:

- WhatsApp integration for patient interaction
- Secure report upload links
- Share appointment details via links
- SMS support (optional/extended)

**Benefits:**

- Structured and secure communication
- Eliminates informal and unsafe data sharing

---

### Offline Mode & Sync

Designed for real-world usage:

- Works offline for key features
- Stores data locally during no connectivity
- Automatically syncs when back online

**Benefits:**

- Reliable in low-connectivity environments
- Ensures uninterrupted workflow

## Data Safety & HIPAA Compliance

MediPraxis prioritizes **data security, privacy, and compliance**.

### Key Security Features:

- End-to-end encryption (data in transit and at rest)
- Secure authentication (OTP-based login)
- Role-based access control
- Secure file storage and handling
- Audit logs for tracking sensitive actions
- Encrypted report sharing via secure links

### Compliance:

- Designed following **HIPAA guidelines**
- Supports **GDPR principles**
- Ensures:
  - Data confidentiality
  - Data integrity
  - Secure access control

### Important Notes:

- Patient data is **never exposed or shared insecurely**
- AI does **NOT use patient data for training**
- All sensitive operations are logged and monitored

## AI Assistant Capabilities

The MediPraxis AI Assistant is designed to **assist practitioners**, not replace them.

### What the AI Assistant CAN do:

#### Documentation Support

- Summarize clinical notes
- Generate structured summaries
- Assist in record creation

#### Data Extraction

- Extract key entities (names, dates, times)
- Identify relevant medical metadata

#### Workflow Assistance

- Help create appointments using voice/text
- Retrieve patient records
- Assist with task management

#### Smart Reminders

- Generate reminders for tasks and schedules
- Provide contextual suggestions

#### Voice Interaction

- Speech-to-text input
- Voice-based commands for actions

---

## AI Assistant Limitations

The AI assistant is intentionally restricted for safety.

### What the AI Assistant DOES NOT do:

- ❌ Make medical diagnoses
- ❌ Provide clinical decisions
- ❌ Replace practitioner judgment
- ❌ Act autonomously without user input
- ❌ Train on or reuse patient data

### Design Principle:

> AI is **supportive, controlled, and human-supervised**
